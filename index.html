02 / Course Details

BAMS Course Details

Ayurveda, one of India’s oldest systems of medicine, focuses on preventing and treating illnesses through natural remedies, with herbal medicine being a key component. Traditionally, this knowledge was imparted to students of noble families through the gurukul system. Today, this time-honoured science is taught through specialised academic programmes offered by several leading Ayurvedic colleges in Karnataka. Among these programmes, the Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery (BAMS) is the most sought-after undergraduate course in Ayurvedic education in India. Now, if you are planning to pursue BAMS after NEET, it is important to understand the fundamentals of the course before beginning the admission process.

05 / Admissions

BAMS Admission Process

01

Entrance Examination

Students aspiring to pursue Ayurvedic courses in Bangalore or elsewhere in India must qualify the National Eligibility-cum-Entrance Test for Undergraduates (NEET-UG) as a mandatory requirement for BAMS admission. In addition, candidates must have completed their 10+2/ 2nd PUC or equivalent examination with Physics, Chemistry, Biology/ Biotechnology, and English as compulsory subjects, securing the minimum qualifying aggregate marks prescribed for their respective category, generally ranging between 40% and 50%.

02

Counselling Process

Candidates who qualify NEET-UG must subsequently participate in the BAMS counselling process, through which seats are allocated at both the All India and state levels. The All India counselling process is conducted by the AYUSH Admissions Central Counselling Committee (AACCC) for admission to Ayurvedic colleges across the country. State-level counselling, on the other hand, is administered by the respective state counselling authorities for admissions within their jurisdiction. For admission to BAMS colleges in Karnataka, the state counselling process is conducted by the Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA).

After securing a seat, candidates will have to report at the allotted college and submit the documents listed below to complete the BAMS admission process.

07 / FAQ

Frequently
Asked
Questions

What is BAMS course duration?
+

The BAMS course duration is 5.5 years. This undergraduate programme has 4.5 years of academic studies followed by a 1-year mandatory internship.

What is the BAMS course eligibility criteria?
+

Candidates must have completed their 10+2/ 2nd PU/ equivalent with English as one of the subjects and must have obtained a minimum aggregate of 40-50% (based on the category of candidate) in Physics, Chemistry & Biology. Candidates must also clear the NEET-UG entrance exam.

Is NEET mandatory for BAMS admission in Karnataka?
+

Yes, qualifying NEET-UG is mandatory for admission to BAMS colleges in Karnataka as well as across India. Candidates must secure the minimum qualifying percentile prescribed for their category to participate in the counselling process.

What is the counselling process for BAMS admission in Karnataka?
+

BAMS admission in Karnataka is conducted through both All India and state-level counselling. The All India counselling process is managed by AACCC, while Karnataka state counselling for BAMS admissions is conducted by the Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA).

What career opportunities are available after completing BAMS?
+

After completing BAMS, graduates can explore career opportunities in clinical practice, Ayurvedic hospitals, wellness centres, pharmaceuticals, research, medical tourism, academics, and healthcare administration. Many students also pursue postgraduate studies for advanced studies.
